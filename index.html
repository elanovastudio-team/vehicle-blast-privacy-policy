1. Introduction

Vehicle Blast respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our mobile game.

2. Information Collection

Vehicle Blast does not require account registration or login. Game progress, scores, coins, power-ups and preferences are stored locally on your device.

We do not operate our own user accounts, backend servers or cloud database for storing game progress.

3. Advertising

Vehicle Blast uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded video advertisements.

AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location.
  • Advertising ID and device identifiers.
  • Advertisement views, clicks and interactions.
  • App usage and diagnostic information.

This information may be used for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service improvement. Data handling is subject to Google's applicable policies and the advertising SDK configuration.

4. Use of Information

Locally stored game information is used to:

  • Save scores and game progress.
  • Maintain coin and power-up balances.
  • Remember player settings.
  • Provide game features and functionality.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. We do not operate our own service for sharing player data. However, Google AdMob may process information through its advertising services.

6. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising and audience settings must be configured according to applicable Google Play and Google AdMob requirements.

7. Data Storage and Deletion

Game data is stored locally on your device. You can delete this local data by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the game.

Advertising-related data is handled by the third-party advertising provider according to its applicable policies.

8. Third-Party Services

Vehicle Blast uses Google AdMob for advertising. Third-party services operate under their own privacy and data-handling practices.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game or its services change. Updated versions will be published on this webpage.
